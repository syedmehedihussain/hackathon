# Legal Tech Challenge - Participant Dataset

All names, companies, contracts, and clauses are fictional.

Files:
- company_standards.json: approved company clause standards
- contracts/: eight contract excerpts in plain text
- public_test_questions.json: questions teams may use during development
- missing_information_cases.json: safe-abstention examples

The prototype must not provide legal advice. Every result must include evidence and require human review.
